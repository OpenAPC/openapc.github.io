The [Free University of Bozen-Bolzano](https://www.unibz.it/) has updated its APC expenditures, the recent contribution provides data for the 2024-2025 period.

Contact person for the data set is [Paolo Buoso](mailto:Paolo.Buoso@unibz.it).


## Cost Data



The new APC data set covers publication fees for 126 articles, total expenditure amounts to 261 242€ and the average fee is 2 073€.
