The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Carl von Ossietzky Universität Oldenburg](https://uol.de/en/bis/research-publishing/open-access/open-access-publication-fund) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Kim Braun](mailto:kim.braun@uni-oldenburg.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 187 articles (84 unrelated to any agreement, 103 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 424,126€ and the average fee is 2,268€.
