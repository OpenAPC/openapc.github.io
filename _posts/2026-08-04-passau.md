The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [University of Passau](https://www.ub.uni-passau.de/publizieren/open-access/publikationsfonds/) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Alena Tenchova-Janzik](mailto:alena.tenchova-janzik@uni-passau.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 6 articles (6 unrelated to any agreement, 0 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 11,817€ and the average fee is 1,970€.

The following table provides an overview.  




|                                                | Articles| Fees paid in EURO| Mean Fee paid|
|:-----------------------------------------------|--------:|-----------------:|-------------:|
|Springer Nature                                 |        3|              5823|          1941|
|Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET) |        1|              2722|          2722|
|MDPI AG                                         |        1|              2380|          2380|
|SDEWES Centre                                   |        1|               892|           892|
