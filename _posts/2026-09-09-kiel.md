The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel](https://www.ub.uni-kiel.de/de/publizieren/open-access-finanzierung) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Dr. Eike Hentschel](mailto:oa-publikationsfonds@ub.uni-kiel.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 103 articles. Total APC expenditure amounts to 264,857€ and the average fee is 2,571€.
