The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Münster University](https://www.uni-muenster.de/Publizieren/dienstleistungen/foerderung/publikationsfonds.html) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Viola Voß](mailto:openaccess@uni-muenster.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 348 articles (157 unrelated to any agreement, 191 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 878,249€ and the average fee is 2,524€.
