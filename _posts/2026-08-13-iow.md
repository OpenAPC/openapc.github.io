The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Leibniz Institute for Baltic Sea Research](https://www.io-warnemuende.de/en_index.html) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Olivia Diehr](mailto:olivia.diehr@io-warnemuende.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 23 articles (17 unrelated to any agreement, 6 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 59,322€ and the average fee is 2,579€.
