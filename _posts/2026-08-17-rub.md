The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Ruhr Universität Bochum](http://www.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/oa/) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Kathrin Lucht-Roussel](mailto:oa@rub.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 305 articles (119 unrelated to any agreement, 186 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 749,072€ and the average fee is 2,456€.
