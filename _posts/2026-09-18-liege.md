The [University of Liège](https://www.uliege.be/) has provided APC expenditures for the 2025 period.

[Liège University Library](https://explore.lib.uliege.be/discovery/search?vid=32ULG_INST:ULIEGE&lang=en) collects data on institutional publication costs and kindly provides this data to OpenAPC.

Contact Person is [Paul Thirion](mailto:Paul.Thirion@uliege.be).

## Cost data



The new data set covers publication fees for 233 articles, total expenditure amounts to 579,055€ and the average fee is 2,485€.
