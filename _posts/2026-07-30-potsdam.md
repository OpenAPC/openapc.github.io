The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [University of Potsdam](https://publishup.uni-potsdam.de/home/index/help/content/publication_fund) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Heike Stadler](mailto:heike.stadler@uni-potsdam.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 143 articles (97 unrelated to any agreement, 46 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 361,690€ and the average fee is 2,529€.
