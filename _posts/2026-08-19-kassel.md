The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [University of Kassel](http://www.uni-kassel.de/go/publikationsfonds) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Dr. Tobias Pohlmann](mailto:openaccess@bibliothek.uni-kassel.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 76 articles (41 unrelated to any agreement, 35 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 165,432€ and the average fee is 2,177€.
