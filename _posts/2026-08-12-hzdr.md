The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf](http://www.hzdr.de/db/Cms?pNid=73) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Maik Fiedler](mailto:maik.fiedler@hzdr.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 54 articles (24 unrelated to any agreement, 30 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 131,040€ and the average fee is 2,427€.
