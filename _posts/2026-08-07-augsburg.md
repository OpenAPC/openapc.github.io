The [University of Augsburg](https://www.uni-augsburg.de/en/) has updated its expenditures, the latest contribution provides TA data for the 2024-2025 period.

Contact person is [Sonja Härkönen](mailto:openaccess@bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 101 articles (published under an agreement like DEAL).




|                                                           | Articles| Fees paid in EURO| Mean Fee paid|
|:----------------------------------------------------------|--------:|-----------------:|-------------:|
|Springer Nature                                            |       58|            123409|          2128|
|Elsevier BV                                                |       28|             47477|          1696|
|Wiley-Blackwell                                            |       14|             28768|          2055|
|American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS) |        1|              2054|          2054|
