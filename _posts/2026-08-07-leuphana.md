The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Leuphana University of Lüneburg](https://www.leuphana.de) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Gesa Baron](mailto:gesa.baron@leuphana.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 31 articles (31 unrelated to any agreement, 0 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 63,231€ and the average fee is 2,040€.
