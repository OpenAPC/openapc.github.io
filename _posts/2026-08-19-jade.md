The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Jade University of Applied Sciences](https://www.jade-hs.de/en/about-us/organisation/library/a-z/open-science/) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated, making the institution participate in OpenAPC for the first time.


## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 6 article (4 unrelated to any agreement, 2 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 9,633€ and the average fee is 1,605€.
