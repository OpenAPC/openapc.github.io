The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Hochschule Anhalt](https://www.hs-anhalt.de/en/university/institutions/library/publish.html) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.


## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 9 articles (7 unrelated to any agreement, 2 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 21,272€ and the average fee is 2,364€.

The following table provides an overview.  




|                | Articles| Fees paid in EURO| Mean Fee paid|
|:---------------|--------:|-----------------:|-------------:|
|MDPI AG         |        5|             14711|          2942|
|Springer Nature |        2|              4103|          2051|
|Cogitatio       |        1|              1130|          1130|
|Elsevier BV     |        1|              1328|          1328|
