The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Leipzig University](https://www.ub.uni-leipzig.de/open-science/open-access-finanzierung/publikationen-in-zeitschriften/publikationsfonds) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Martin Bauschmann](mailto:openscience@ub.uni-leipzig.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 377 articles (375 unrelated to any agreement, 2 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 911,760€ and the average fee is 2,418€.
