The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [KIT Karlsruhe](https://www.bibliothek.kit.edu/publikationsfonds.php) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Rhiannon Schmitt](mailto:openaccess@bibliothek.kit.edu).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 386 articles (215 unrelated to any agreement, 171 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 845,462€ and the average fee is 2,190€.
