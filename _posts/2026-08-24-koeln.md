The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Universität zu Köln](https://ub.uni-koeln.de/en/publishing-research/publikationsfonds) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Inga Frieman](mailto:frieman@ub.uni-koeln.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 223 articles (223 unrelated to any agreement, 0 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 563,166€ and the average fee is 2,525€.
