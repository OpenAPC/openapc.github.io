The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Nürtingen-Geislingen University of Applied Science](https://www.hfwu.de) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Elena Kubein](mailto:openaccess@hfwu.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 4 articles (2 unrelated to any agreement, 2 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 6,893€ and the average fee is 1,723€.

The following table provides an overview.  




|                       | Articles| Fees paid in EURO| Mean Fee paid|

|Elsevier BV            |        1|              1233|          1233|
|MDPI AG                |        1|              1765|          1765|
|Springer Nature        |        1|              1159|          1159|
|Walter de Gruyter GmbH |        1|              2737|          2737|
