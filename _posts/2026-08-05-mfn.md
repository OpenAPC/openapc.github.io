The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Museum für Naturkunde - Leibniz-Institut für Evolutions- und Biodiversitätsforschung](https://www.museumfuernaturkunde.berlin/forschung/sammlung/bibliothek) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Stefanie Paß](mailto:Stefanie.Pass@mfn.berlin).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 17 articles (14 unrelated to any agreement, 3 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 25,536€ and the average fee is 1,502€.
