The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [University of Mannheim](https://www.bib.uni-mannheim.de/en/open-access-publishing-fund/) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Philipp Zumstein](mailto:philipp.zumstein@uni-mannheim.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 22 articles (22 unrelated to any agreement, 0 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 36,861€ and the average fee is 1,676€.
