The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Paul-Drude-Institut für Festkörperelektronik](https://www.pdi-berlin.de/) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Anne Timm](mailto:timm@pdi-berlin.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 9 articles (3 unrelated to any agreement, 6 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 27,213€ and the average fee is 3,024€.

The following table provides an overview.  




|                                                       | Articles| Fees paid in EURO| Mean Fee paid|

|AIP Publishing                                         |        4|              9095|          2274|

|Springer Nature                                        |        2|             10748|          5374|
|Institute of Electrical & Electronics Engineers (IEEE) |        1|              2340|          2340|
