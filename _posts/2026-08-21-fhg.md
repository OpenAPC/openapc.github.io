The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft zur Förderung der angewandten Forschung e. V.](https://www.openaccess.fraunhofer.de/en/open-access-strategy.html) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Stefanie Seeh](mailto:stefanie.seeh@zv.fraunhofer.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 396 articles (396 unrelated to any agreement, 0 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 729,410€ and the average fee is 1,842€.
