The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [INM - Leibniz Institute for New Materials](https://www.ntnm-bib.de/en/) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Elke Bubel](mailto:elke.bubel@leibniz-inm.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 15 articles (3 unrelated to any agreement, 12 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 36,565€ and the average fee is 2,438€.

The following table provides an overview.

|Springer Nature                                        |        3|              5725|          1908|
|American Chemical Society (ACS)                        |        2|              7389|          3694|
|Elsevier BV                                            |        1|              2500|          2500|
|Institute of Electrical & Electronics Engineers (IEEE) |        1|              2410|          2410|
