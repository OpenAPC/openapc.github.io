The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron DESY](https://library.desy.de/publish/index_eng.html) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Alexander Wagner](mailto:alexander.wagner@desy.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 63 articles (31 unrelated to any agreement, 32 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 159,401€ and the average fee is 2,530€.
