The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Hochschule Kaiserslautern](https://www.hs-kl.de/en/forschung/weiteres-zur-forschung/publikationsfonds) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.


## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 6 article (6 unrelated to any agreement, 0 published under an agreement like DEAL).




|                                     | Articles| Fees paid in EURO| Mean Fee paid|
|:------------------------------------|--------:|-----------------:|-------------:|
|MDPI AG                              |        3|              3171|          1057|
|American Physiological Society       |        1|              3012|          3012|
|Oxford University Press (OUP)        |        1|              2200|          2200|
|Scientific Research Publishing, Inc. |        1|               343|           343|
