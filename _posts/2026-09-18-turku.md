The [University of Turku](https://www.utu.fi/en) has updated its APC expenditures, the recent contribution provides cost data on APCs for the fourth quarter of 2025 and the first quarter of 2026.

The data has been collected by [Turku University Library](https://www.utu.fi/en/university/library), contact person is [Mikko Pennanen](mailto:mikko.pennanen@utu.fi).


## Cost data



The new data set covers publication fees for 92 articles. Total expenditure amounts to 222,930€ and the average fee is 2,423€.
