The [University of Ferrara](https://www.unife.it/it) has kindly provided APC and BPC expenditures for the 2025 period.

Institutional contact for the data set is the University's [Bibliometric Office](mailto:ub@unife.it).

## Cost data



The new data set covers publication fees for 110 articles, total expenditure amounts to 281,066€ and the average fee is 2,555€.

The following table provides an overview of the new data. Please note that BPCs are not included in this list, as they are aggregated in OpenAPC's [BPC](https://github.com/OpenAPC/openapc-de/blob/master/data/bpc.csv) dataset.
