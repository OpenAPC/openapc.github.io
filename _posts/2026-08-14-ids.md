The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Leibniz Institute for the German Language](https://www.ids-mannheim.de/bibliothek/open-access-am-ids/) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Monika Pohlschmidt](mailto:oa@ids-mannheim.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 4 articles (1 unrelated to any agreement, 3 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 5,489€ and the average fee is 1,372€.

The following table provides an overview.  




|                       | Articles| Fees paid in EURO| Mean Fee paid|
|:----------------------|--------:|-----------------:|-------------:|
|Springer Nature        |        2|              2111|          1055|
|MDPI AG                |        1|              2950|          2950|
|Walter de Gruyter GmbH |        1|               428|           428|
