The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Deutsches Primatenzentrum - Leibniz-Institut für Primatenforschung](https://www.dpz.eu/de/abteilung/bibliothek/serviceangebot.html) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.


## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 31 articles (31 unrelated to any agreement, none published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 81,933€ and the average fee is 2,643€.
