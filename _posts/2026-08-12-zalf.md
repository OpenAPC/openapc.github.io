The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Leibniz-Zentrum für Agrarlandschaftsforschung (ZALF)](https://www.zalf.de/en/struktur/bib/Pages/Bibliothek.aspx) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Questions regarding the data should be directed at the [ZALF library](mailto:bib@zalf.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 65 articles (34 unrelated to any agreement, 31 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 131,724€ and the average fee is 2,027€.
