The [Utrecht University Library](https://www.uu.nl/en/university-library) collects data on APC expenditures from [Utrecht University](https://www.uu.nl/en) and the [University Medical Center Utrecht](https://www.umcutrecht.nl/en). 

A data set on APC and BPC expenditures for the 2019 to 2025 period has been kindly made available to OpenAPC.

Contact person is [Jan de Boer](mailto:J.deBoer@uu.nl).

## Cost data



The data set covers publication fees for 3,809 articles, total expenditure amounts to 9,210,663€ and the average fee is 2,418€.
The following table provides an overview of the new data. Please note that BPCs are not included in this list, as they are aggregated in OpenAPC's [BPC](https://github.com/OpenAPC/openapc-de/blob/master/data/bpc.csv) dataset.
