The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Leibniz-Zentrum für Marine Tropenforschung](https://www.leibniz-zmt.de/en/research-infrastructure/media-unit.html) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.

Contact person for the data set is [Clara Bruns](mailto:clara.bruns@leibniz-zmt.de).

## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 16 articles (9 unrelated to any agreement, 7 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 32,357€ and the average fee is 2,022€.
