The Forschungszentrum Jülich collects publication cost data from German institutions as part of the DFG programme "[Open Access Publication Funding](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/zb/open-science/open-access/monitoring-dfg-oa-publication-funding)".

When reporting their publication costs, the [Kiel Institute for the World Economy](https://www.ifw-kiel.de/institute/about-the-kiel-institute/organization/open-access-policy-of-the-kiel-institute/) had agreed to share the data with OpenAPC as well. This data has now been transferred and integrated.


## Cost data



The new data covers APCs for 3 articles (None unrelated to any agreement, 3 published under an agreement like DEAL). Total APC expenditure amounts to 4,796€ and the average fee is 1,599€.

The following table provides an overview.  




|                | Articles| Fees paid in EURO| Mean Fee paid|
|:---------------|--------:|-----------------:|-------------:|
|Springer Nature |        3|              4796|          1599|
